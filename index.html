Known data quality issues - read before quoting these numbers
1. Connect-to-DCD is unreliable in its current form. DCD is read from a CRM status field while Connected comes from call logs, so the two do not share a denominator. 84 attendees carry DCD_Flag = 1 with zero logged two-way calls - 56 of them have a DCD_Date that predates their webinar, meaning the tag was carried forward from an earlier programme cycle. This inflates Connect-to-DCD and produces the five domain rows above 100% in the Domain-Level Breakdown.

Impact on the headline: the current figures show 5-8 at 65% versus 16-20 at 92%, a 27 percentage point gap. Applying the clean definition (DCD_Flag = 1 AND Connected = 1) gives 5-8 at 55% and 16-20 at 62% - a 7 point gap. The direction holds but the magnitude does not. Do not quote the 27 point gap externally.

Clean rates if recomputed: 3-5 at 60%, 5-8 at 55%, 9-15 at 68%, 16-20 at 62%, 20+ at 52%. Total DCD count drops from 340 to 256.

Fix at source: stamp DCD_Date only on a call logged after the current webinar, or add a DCD_source field so carried-forward tags stay distinguishable. Until then use the AND condition.
2. Two different Enrolled numbers exist in this dataset. Every figure in this dashboard is FDE-only - 27 confirmed FDE Program India sales cross-referenced by HubSpot ID. The all-programme rate is materially higher because 21 attendees enrolled in other IK programmes, mostly Transformative GenAI & AgenticAI variants.

FDE-only versus all-programme by cohort: 5-8 is 2.3% vs 2.8% - 9-15 is 2.7% vs 5.8% - 16-20 is 3.3% vs 9.9% - 20+ is 2.0% vs 10.0%. Overall 2.4% vs 5.6%.

Never place both on the same chart without labels. Suggested naming: FDE_Conversion_Rate and Webinar_Total_Conversion_Rate. Note also that 16 of the 21 other-programme enrollments have a blank course field and cannot be verified from this sheet alone.

Worth a separate look: 16-20 converts at 9.9% across all programmes but only 3.3% into FDE. That band is buying - just not buying FDE.

    Summary - Key Observations

    Scope: 3-5 YOE is attendees only. All other cohorts include the full connected population. 661 attendees in the funnel view, 582 transcripts in the call view, 16 FDE enrolled within attendee scope, 27 FDE sales in total across all funnels.
    1. 47% of revenue leakage is Sales process, not lead quality or offer. Demand quality accounts for 45%, offer only 9%. Within the positive pipeline, Sales process rises to 52%. The leads showed up; the follow-through did not.
    2. 77% of all calls end without a next step and 47% carry an unresolved objection. Only 11% of objections get resolved on the call. Enrolled leads had a next step 80% of the time. This is the single most consistent gap across every cohort, channel and segment.
    3. 5-8 and 9-15 are nearly identical on call quality. Intent, FDE interest, leakage split and role fit all sit within five percentage points. The one real gap is objection resolution: 7% for 5-8 versus 16% for 9-15.
    4. The 5-8 shortfall is a channel problem. 5-8 Talent 500: 131 leads, zero FDE enrollments. 5-8 non-T500: 46 leads, 4 enrollments (8.7%). All four came from Organic, Google Brand and Google NB. In transcripts, T500 5-8 calls reach pricing 33% of the time versus 54% for non-T500.
    5. The positive pipeline does not behave like buyers. Pricing discussed 54% versus 87% for enrolled, next step 28% versus 80%, immediate urgency 13% versus 60%. The weekly pipeline number overstates real intent.
    6. Lead tiering separates buyers cleanly. 202 TOP, 157 MIDDLE, 205 LOW. 14 of 15 enrolled leads score TOP; all 120 do-not-pursue leads score LOW. 68 TOP-tier leads are currently sitting in NOT_INTERESTED - they were given up on too early.
    7. 231 leads are recoverable now (14 P0 call-now plus 217 P1 follow-up), with 213 more on nurture. Only 120 are genuinely dead. 72% of everything analysed is conditionally recoverable.
    8. Intent, not price, is the terminal blocker. "No plan" accounts for 37-39% of connected leads; pricing objection is 2-6%. Top objection themes: pricing 24%, coding concern 17%, AI/ML overlap 15%, ROI 13%, seniority 11%.
