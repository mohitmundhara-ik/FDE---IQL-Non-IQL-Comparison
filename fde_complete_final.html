Watch out
"No plan" is the dominant terminal state at 37-39% across all cohorts. This is not a pricing problem - pricing objection is only 2-6% everywhere. The webinar is attracting people who are curious about FDE as a concept but are not actively looking to transition. They attend, they listen, they may even pick up when Sales calls - but they have no actionable career-change plan. This has implications for webinar targeting: consider qualifying for active career-transition signals (currently interviewing, applied to roles, updated resume) at registration.
